Regen resolves before Burn, then durations expire. Bleed resolves before the bearer acts. Boss damage over time is capped at 6% of maximum HP each round.

Four reactions

  • Crumb Burst: detonate a target carrying Crumb.
  • Wildfire: hit a target with three Burn stacks using Splash, Row or All. Nearby foes gain Burn.
  • Rupture: directly hit a target with Bleed and Shred. Bleed is consumed for damage through shields.
  • Spread: defeat a Marked target. Its debuffs move to the nearest foe at half stacks, rounded up.

A target triggers at most one reaction per round. Reaction damage cannot start another reaction.
