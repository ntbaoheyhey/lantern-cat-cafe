2. What the reference videos teach OMG 3Q, learn only

Studied: the 45-minute newbie guide (frame every 12 s) and the 15-minute battle video (frame every 3 s, plus two slow-motion bursts at 5 frames per second and an audio spectrogram). We copy structure and timing, never art, names or assets, and never feed their frames to any generator.

2.1 Anatomy of one ultimate (measured)

Battle field

  • 6 per side in two staggered rows; units ≈22-25% of screen height; overlap 20-40%.
  • Centre gap only ≈10-15% of width; statues frame both edges, so no empty floor.
  • Normal hits stay on the field (dash, crescent slash, sparks), 1-1.5 s.
  • Summons (phoenix, tiger, serpent) give each ultimate its own identity.
  • Element colour language: ice cyan-white, fire orange spiral, thunder violet, nature green, holy gold pillars.

Battle HUD

  • Thin top strip: turn counter, wave.
  • Bottom: one row of square bust portraits with HP + rage micro-bars; gold glow = ultimate ready; greyscale = dead.
  • Big "x3" speed bottom-left, Skip bottom-right.
  • Over units: small HP bar + 1-2 status glyphs; auras on the body.
  • Huge italic outlined damage numbers; "total damage" ribbon.
  • Anti-pattern: piles of floating buff words. We use icons and one short word at most.

Out of battle

  • Login: full-bleed key art, logo, one big orange CTA.
  • Home: heroes standing large on a stage; currencies top-right with "+"; painted event icons with red dots; bottom dock.
  • Panels: dark navy/plum, gold bevel frames, ribbon titles, orange buttons, rarity-framed item tiles.
  • Hero page: art on a pedestal, stats with green deltas, tabs; star-chart upgrades.
  • Stages: 3 stars, sweep ×10, drop preview, stamina.

Economy patterns

  • Hero and gear shards bought in mode shops with daily limits and timed refresh.
  • Dismantle returns materials; one currency per mode (arena honour, tower soul).
  • 7-day launch login track, level milestone gifts, VIP track.
  • World boss with a daily time window and damage ranking.
  • Every title in a heavy display face with gradient and outline. No plain fonts anywhere.

Audio: the spectrogram shows continuous music plus dense hit sounds with almost no silence, and a voice line roughly every 7-8 s in auto battle, timed with skill casts. The audio is only read as a picture here, so we know density and cadence, not the words.

3. Direction

Lantern Café: a cozy-but-fierce night café defended by a squad of cats. Turn-based, readable, short sessions, with an economy where real items are earned, fused and burned on Avalanche.

1. Battles that look like a real game

Dense two-row formation, equal core sizes, a clean HUD, and ultimates that take over the screen for 3-4 seconds. Each ultimate differs in palette, camera, motif, target shape and sound.

2. Sound everywhere

Music never stops. Every hit, status and button has a sound. Every cat has a voice and an ultimate line.

3. Visibly on-chain

A web3 landing page with live Fuji counters, wallet or guest sign-in, a Genesis Café Deed, bonded rewards you can withdraw, and fusion that burns real NFTs with Snowtrace links.

4. Fair, published economy

No token per battle, randomness only with bound currency, every probability on screen, sinks larger than faucets, and FOMO on cosmetics and editions, not on power.

4. Battle look

4.1 Formation

Rules keep a team of 4 (balance already passes). The layout changes to two dense staggered rows like the reference. A move to 5-6 units is a post-contest roadmap item because it would reopen balance.

  • Unit height 0.31 → 0.42 of the stage (+35%). Overlap 15-25%, depth-sorted by feet; back row at 94% scale and 8% darker.
  • Camera: lower horizon so the floor fills ~70% of the frame; tighter margins; painted props (counter end, plant pots, cup stacks) at both edges.
  • Core size rule: the "core box" (top of head to feet, excluding ears, hats, weapons, tails, wings) is identical for every cat and normal enemy; elites 1.15×, bosses 1.7-1.95×. A script fails the build if any cat is off by more than 2%.
  • Soft contact shadow and a thin element-coloured ring under every unit.

5.2 How Codex can make it look good (the process)

  1. Research first (90 min, written down): versions of three.js, the Spine runtime, postprocessing and three.quarks; headless rendering speed; 10 timing rules from anime VFX craft (impact frames, smears, hitstop).
  2. One sequence format (SkillSequence JSON) with tracks for camera, time scale, dim, cut-in, caster/target animation, VFX layers, post effects, sound cues, damage splits and the plate. Impact time stays bound to the rules engine so results never change.
  3. Cinematic compositor: a small page driven by a plate JSON (background, speed-line shader, the cat's own art as a giant glowing silhouette, flipbooks, particles, flashes, a one-frame inverted impact frame, colour grade, a summoned creature). Headless Chromium renders each frame at a fixed timestep; ffmpeg encodes WebM (and MP4 fallback); frames are deleted after encoding. Fixes are cheap: edit JSON, re-render.
  4. Distinctness rule: each ultimate has a signature card with 5 fields (element palette, camera preset, motif, target shape, sound kit). A script fails if two ultimates share 3 or more.
  5. Pilot gate: 3 pilots (Mochi shield dome, Soot arcane fire, Clover nature) with review clips and a VFX lab page; you approve or comment in a text file. Codex keeps working on actives meanwhile and proceeds after 24 h without an answer.
  6. Batch 12 cats + boss. Cut line on 17 Nov: every cat gets at least cut-in + field impact; the 6 most-used get full plates.

Summon ideas that fit our world: a giant yarn whale, a teapot dragon, a flock of paper cranes, a ghost cat, a rain of sewing needles, a petal storm, an ink splash.

Player settings: "Ultimate cinematics: Full / Short / Off", auto-Short after seeing the same plate 3 times in a session, and a Skip button.

8. Fonts and icons

Fonts

  • Display: Baloo 2 ExtraBold (Vietnamese marks supported), always with a gold-to-orange gradient, dark outline and drop shadow.
  • Body: a condensed face with full Vietnamese (Be Vietnam Pro or Barlow Condensed, verified glyph by glyph).
  • Numbers: baked digit atlas with outline for the battle.
  • A lint rule forbids any font outside the token file.

Icons

  • One style: 128 px master, chunky painted, 3 px dark outline, top-left light, slight 3/4 view.
  • SVG for status and navigation (crisp at 18-24 px); painted for items, gear, currencies.
  • Rarity frames as separate layers: grey, green, blue, purple, gold, red-gold Mythic.
  • Tiles: icon + frame + count + tags (new, shard, sealed-on-chain).
  • All unicode glyph icons removed.

11. How a player starts decision

Decision: free to play, earning gated by a Genesis Café Deed. Games that required an NFT to play lost most players at the wallet step; games that gate earning (Big Time, Axie's new bonded rewards, MapleStory Universe's level gate) keep the fun open and give the NFT a real job.

Genesis Café Deed (NFT)

  • 3,000 ever; RelicNFT ids 30001-33000; no new contract.
  • Contest: free mint with a live "N / 3,000" counter. Launch: auction or fixed price in AVAX.
  • Unlocks withdrawing Spool to YARN; hosts 3-6 free players who share 20% of what they withdraw; café skin, title, +1 seal per day, early edition access.
  • Tiers 1→5 by burning YARN + Star Thread. No combat power.

Tokens

  • YARN: ERC-20 on Fuji, 1B fixed, burnable (deployed).
  • Spool: bonded YARN kept off-chain; all rewards pay in Spool; spend 1:1 in game; withdrawing costs 25% (down to 10% with Deed tier and good standing). The fee is never minted.
  • Fish, Thread, Bell: bound soft currencies (9 merged into 3), plus Boss Seal.
  • Star Thread: rare material, sealable and tradable.

12. Gameplay, what makes it special, and the hooks

Core loop (5-10 min)

Pick 4 cats and their row → read enemy intents → fight with rage-driven ultimates → loot gear and Fish → enhance or fuse at the Forge → push the next chapter node.

What is ours

Intent reading (you see what each enemy will do next turn and can retarget, shield or interrupt), the café night setting, cats with cut-in ultimates, and items that become real NFTs only through effort and a burn.

Daily

One Boss Seal fight, dailies (Bells), idle café income (12 h cap), diminishing drops (full rate for 6 drops, then 50% to 18, then Fish/Thread only).

Weekly and season (4 weeks)

World Boss with shared HP and damage ranking (roadmap), arena ladder, featured cat banner, season skin line, limited Relic edition, season pass.

Time-limited (FOMO)Never time-limited (fair)
Season skins and titles · season Relic edition with an on-chain supply counter · Genesis Deed (3,000 ever) · Mythic cap of 500 per season · featured banner window · first-clear titlesCats (reruns + selector) · gear power (drops + fusion) · story chapters · enhancement

How a player earns: season rank, boss rank and milestones pay Spool; withdraw to YARN with a Deed; sell sealed Epic+ gear, Star Thread, editions and Deeds on the market (5% fee: 2% burned). Buying never gives power beyond what a free player can reach.

  • Sealing (off-chain item → NFT): level gate, 3 per day (+1 with Deed), costs 1 Boss Seal and a burned fee (10% of 100 / 300 / 900 YARN reference for Epic / Legendary / Mythic; 20 for Star Thread), 10-minute relist delay.

14. Economy, rates and price effects

14.1 Ten rules taken from big projects

  1. Every loop is fun with the token switched off (lesson of Hamster Kombat and STEPN).
  2. Three layers: bound soft currency → bonded Spool → fixed-supply YARN (Axie bAXS, Pixels vPIXEL).
  3. No token per battle; rewards come from a fixed season budget with an automatic cut (already deployed in SeasonRewards).
  4. Tradables come only from effort plus a burn (Off The Grid, Gods Unchained).
  5. Every tradable type has an item sink, not only a token sink (Splinterlands overprint).
  6. Level and quest gate before anything goes on-chain (MapleStory Universe).
  7. YARN never buys a random result.
  8. Time-limited content is cosmetic; power stays reachable.
  9. Publish every probability next to its button (Korea 2024 law).
  10. Measure burn/emission, YARN per active hour and top-1% share every season; auto-cut below 0.8.

14.6 Player-to-player trading and prices

  • Supply = sealing (level gate, Boss Seal, daily cap) + fusion output. Demand = fusion inputs (recurring), players saving time, collectors of Perfect quality and editions, Deed upgrades (Star Thread).
  • Recipes tie the tiers: P(Legendary) ≈ 3·P(Epic) + 2·P(Star Thread) + 20, and P(Mythic) ≈ 2·P(Legendary) + 5·P(Star Thread) + 80. If Epics crash, players buy and fuse them, which removes Epics and lifts the price; if Legendaries spike, fusion adds supply. No price floor or ceiling is needed.
  • Risks: bots farming Epics (level gate, Boss Seal, daily cap, reputation-based fees, flags on round trips); wash trading (5% fee, no volume rewards); post-season crashes (old editions become café décor sets; Mythic cap); whales (Mythic +5% max, arena brackets by power).
  • KPIs per season: burn/emission ≥ 1.0, withdraw rate, YARN per active hour (casual vs heavy ≤ 3×), top-1% Spool share, fused/sealed ≥ 0.5, Star Thread source/sink 0.9-1.1, median price per tier, D1/D7/D30, featured cat reached by ≥ 90% of daily players.
  • Allocation (kept as deployed): players 35%, team 15%, treasury 12%, investors 12%, ecosystem 10%, early testers 6%, liquidity 5%, advisors 3%, partners 2%.
