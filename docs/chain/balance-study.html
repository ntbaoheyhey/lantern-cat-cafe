Closed-season rewards and inventory

Boss rewards are weekly. Rank closes every 13 weeks and claims start the following season (weeks 14/27/40/53). The fourth rank remains pending at week 52. Rank consumes the same reduced 12-YARN weekly cap, with priority; any remainder expires at next close. No per-battle payment.

Eligible source equipment uses the real 6-drop/day cap; no recurring tradable rare material was found. 240-slot eligible shelf and nonadopters are tracked. A buyer stops at 4 owned items and a sealer stops at 6 unsold/owned items. The 6-item preference is a hypothesis, not a protocol cap; stress cases use 2/24/1000. Generic 20-YARN sales and willingness to seal are assumptions, not fair prices. Bots do not buy cosmetic utility or seal a 30-YARN item to net 19.
