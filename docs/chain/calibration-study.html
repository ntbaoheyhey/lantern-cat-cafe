What actually changed

Each new season can commit at most25/50/75% of eligible burns from the previous closed season. Rank receives at most10% of the envelope; the remaining maximum boss amount is quoted equally before the season. Missed completions do not create extra budget. A demand collapse preserves this season’s quote and reduces the next season. Exact integer arithmetic respects the existing on-chain cap.

Opt-in changes who asks for a20-YARN grant, not its amount: all adopters,all bot wallets and5% of other nonadopters. It is a sensitivity assumption, not a working Sybil filter. Bots are known perfectly in the model for burn classification; actual evidence is weaker.

Why lower rewards alone cannot repair inactive grants

claims = burn + player balances + treasury. Passing claims/burn≤2 requires retained balances plus treasury to be no greater than burns. Nonspenders retain their grants. Cutting future rewards cannot remove already issued balances, and a zero-activity economy is not a pass.

There is also a deployed recovery limit: a season with emissions and zero burn makes the future routine cap permanently zero. This study preserves that behavior. A new issuer cannot restore it.
