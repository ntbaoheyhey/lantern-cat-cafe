# Slides Outline

Slide 1: Taskbar Hero - Lantern Cat Cafe, a cosy cat card RPG in the browser with Avalanche Fuji contracts.
Slide 2: The Story: The Lantern Cat Cafe is the last lit shop on a street knotted up by the Tangle and the Yarn Giant.
Slide 3: The Heroes: Mochi (tank, shield + lantern), Clover (healer, teapot), and Soot (mage, purple cloak).
Slide 4: Core Gameplay: Tactical turn-based card battles combining stamina economy, lane defense, and team synergies.
Slide 5: Ultimate Showdowns: Cinematic ultimate abilities—Last Lantern Vow, Fourleaf Spring Brew, and Chimney Comet.
Slide 6: Boss Battle: An intense, multi-phase encounter against the towering Yarn Giant woven from abandoned tasks.
Slide 7: Frictionless Web3 Onboarding: Instant guest sign-in using SIWE with sponsored gas for zero player friction.
Slide 8: Permanent Cafe Relics: Victorious cafe memories minted on Avalanche Fuji as ERC-1155 tokens that cannot be erased.
Slide 9: The On-Chain Economy: In-game market with YARN ERC-20 tokens, fully auditable and verified on Snowtrace.
Slide 10: Technical Architecture: Browser-first Phaser/Three.js rendering engine paired with Avalanche Fuji smart contracts.
