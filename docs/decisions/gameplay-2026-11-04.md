No drop chance, XP multiplier, key cap, crafting price, rarity weight, boss damage, replay event time or server-verified grant was changed. New reward-generating micro-events, interactive boss mechanics, defeat slow-motion and original audio are deferred. Unused speculative tunables were removed rather than shipped as incomplete features. Existing Sample systems remain default-OFF.

The one early claim may consume RNG at a different accepted-claim boundary. Simulations compare identical schedules against the actual server reducers, including duplicate/early requests; they do not claim identical item rolls to the old30s schedule. The measured Fish total is unchanged, but XP/Yarn and item rolls differ for the recorded seed.
