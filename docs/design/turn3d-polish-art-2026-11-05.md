Stitch lines, eyebrow/mouth poses and asymmetrical locks make faces recognizable. Figure details stay below the eyes.

## Stage and light
Closer gentle perspective, front row fills the table. Warm key, cool rim, dark far props and atmospheric falloff separate silhouettes. Procedural wood grain and cloth weave anchor the cafe; glossy cups and a brass lantern provide material contrast. A soft contact shadow sits beneath each figure. Coherent matte fabric, restrained ceramic highlights, baked vertex AO and rim accents. Labels live in reserved screen lanes with measured collision handling and leaders to actors; no card sits over a face.

## Motion and sockets
Named spine/head/ear/tail/limb hierarchy, authored eased keyframes, breathing and secondary ear/tail/cape follow-through. At least fifteen hero clips and eight enemy clips. Head, back, hands, neck and tail sockets accept original modular gear. Gear must change silhouette as well as rarity color. Every asset records script, parameters, author/date, hash, bytes and triangle count; limits: cats 25k, foes 10k, elite 20k, boss 60k, scene 150k triangles / 250 draws.
