The evidence supports a few broad choices, not copied balance tables: legible slot roles, conditional behavior, flexible equipment changes, useful duplicate sinks and a targeted route. We retain four existing slots/eight sets, guaranteed enhancement, chosen affix replacement and targeted tailoring; add full loadouts and comparison text. A new Uncommon tier is not justified: it would create another collection wall without a distinct behavior. Common/Rare/Epic/Legendary remain separate from a zero-stat collectible edition.

These are deliberate limits: no current exact inventory/binding table was verified for the six external games; their gaps remain U in the matrix. Our cap, account-bound rules, colour labels and ceremonies are original design decisions to be tested locally. No external design's scarcity, retention or market price is treated as a demonstrated causal result.

Additional original design consequence: saved loadouts should reference owned instance IDs, show missing pieces before application, and commit atomically. Acquisition at capacity must disclose whether it is rejected or converted before any earned resources are spent. This is our inference from V4-RAID-PRESETS/FORGE, not a claim about their internal transaction code.
