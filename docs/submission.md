# Lantern Cat Café

Lantern Cat Café is a browser RPG about three cats protecting the last lit shop on their street. Play through Chapter 1 to earn a Moon Collar and mint it as an ERC-1155 Relic on Avalanche Fuji. RelicMarketV2 lets two players trade the item and the seller withdraw the proceeds.
