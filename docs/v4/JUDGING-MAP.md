## Seven steps to show

1. Landing and genuine Fuji counters, including the age label when cached.
2. Play free and Start trial, counting clicks and elapsed time.
3. A real battle followed by its earned reward.
4. Mint trial reward, then show time, AVAX fee, actual payer and Snowtrace receipt.
5. Café/workshop: fund, seal, forge, list, buy and Spool withdrawal with 25% retention.
6. Boss fight with melee/ranged/healer labels and the actual mix.
7. My café on Avalanche, wallet-filtered history and a real public Snowtrace page.

The film must label local Anvil footage as local; Anvil transaction hashes are not Fuji proof. A public explorer view must use a separately verified Fuji receipt and identify that provenance. The required offline review build must say “offline / mock chain data” on screen; it is separate from the production rehearsal.
