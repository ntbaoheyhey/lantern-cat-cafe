# Crumb Sentinel visual review
REFERENCE: filmstrips/enemy-crumb-sentinel-basic.webp; filmstrips/enemy-crumb-sentinel.webp; before

Viewed both eight-frame strips at native1280×720. The basic has a small forward pinch followed by a separate large flying sugar shape; the signature uses a sideward crown-shaped burst and a different arm/body arc. The tall paper crown, crossed stitching, orange feet and pink utensil remain readable.

Tier stays **improved**. The body reuses the inherited stitched-opponent family, facial detail is slight, and bright cream additive effects flatten into nearly solid silhouettes. The modest body movement is much less legible than the effects at this projection size. Two distinct motion trajectories and grayscale comparisons do not establish slice-quality art.

The caster-focused crops cut HP plates and distant effects; those cutouts are harness framing, not proof of full-stage clipping. Full-stage gallery review and timing remain separate checks. This visual note is not an opening-performance verdict.
