# Q6 visual critique

1. The selected hero now anchors growth, skills, gear and bonds; small stat deltas remain less immediate than the reference pattern.
2. Bond portraits identify the actual pair, but the relationship still reads as a compact card rather than a wider illustrated network.
3. The Fuji account panel now gives AVAX test gas and YARN separate labelled tiles and four large action tiles.
4. Wallet history names decoded receipt events; its repeated rows are clear but less lively than the reference reward presentations.
5. Fusion and ornament crafting have illustrated inputs and an output focus; existing market ornaments retain their older pixel style.
6. Supplies now has a pantry rail and a selected item or recipient; unavailable actions remain visibly disabled with an explanation.
7. The weekly season track shows real bound rewards and claim state; its repeated cards could gain more individual artwork.
8. Preparation and result controls are readable; the preparation board deliberately leaves space around the staggered squad.
9. Heavy gold frames and repeated mascot poses still make some menus feel more uniform than the reference; empty decorative speech bubbles could be simplified.
10. Withdrawal quotes preserve full numeric precision, which is harder to scan than rounded display values with exact details available on demand.
