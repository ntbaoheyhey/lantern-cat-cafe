# Economy UI review — published candidate

1. The workshop frame and painted navigation make the policy page visually consistent with the café.
2. The current grant, withdrawal retention and fusion cost read clearly at the smaller desktop size.
3. The three large flow cards establish an order, but explicit arrows could make that relationship easier to learn.
4. The mascot creates a clear entry point; its empty speech bubble remains a decorative weakness.
5. Craft prices use currency icons and match the policy exactly in both desktop captures.
6. The permanent benefit labels are short, although newcomers still need help to distinguish the two Thread currencies.
7. The backdrop competes less with the foreground after darkening, but it still shows through the card centers.
8. Odds and fee details are accessible through separate tabs, keeping the main view readable.
9. The bottom counter strip still depends too much on icon recognition; labeling belongs to the remaining screen pass.
10. The local transaction proof is complete; public Fuji availability and the final sensitivity result remain separate work.
