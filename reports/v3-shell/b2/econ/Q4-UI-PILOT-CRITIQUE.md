# Economy UI pilot critique
1. Painted cards and category icons make the transaction stages easy to distinguish.
2. The central dialog occupies a useful desktop area at both requested sizes.
3. The cat guide creates a visual entry point without covering the flow cards.
4. Rates and costs use consistent numeric typography, but small subtitles still need careful reading.
5. Recipe cards show both resource icons beside their starting costs.
6. Guaranteed success is visible; the selected rebate policy still requires a fresh capture.
7. The fee panel separates retained withdrawal fees from market burns and treasury income.
8. Banner odds are aligned in a compact table with independent pity columns.
9. The live counters remain terse and one is unavailable; Q8 must provide real Fuji evidence.
10. These default-policy images demonstrate layout only; final economics will be recaptured after selection.
