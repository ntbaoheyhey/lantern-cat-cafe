# S5 — Quiet, synchronized game audio

Question: what target, source budget and scheduling method make this desktop battle readable, with distinct class weapons and a clear transaction chime?

## Research before production edits

ASWG-R001 v1.2 recommends home output -24 LKFS +/-2 and portable output -18 +/-2, true peak no higher than -1dBTP, measured over at least30minutes of representative gameplay. ITU-T H.872 uses -23LUFS +/-2 for general gameplay and limits its -18 exception to portable built-in speakers. The work order is desktop-only, so -18 is not the appropriate evidence-based default. [S5-SONY,S5-ITU]

Adopt -23LUFS +/-2 for the three fixture diagnostics, retaining <=-1dBTP, LRA>=5LU, 4–8dB voice duck, <=6 simultaneous sources, +/-33ms visual/onset tolerance, <=300ms UI effects and10/12 intelligible lines. This is the research-authorized target correction requested by S5, not a claim of standards compliance. Final representative30-minute output will be measured separately; a60-second fixture cannot establish that standard. LRA>=5 and the concurrency/sync thresholds remain product acceptance requirements rather than claims that EBU mandates them. [S5-ITU,S5-SONY,S5-EBU]

Preload and decode assets before their cues. Use the audio clock for source starts and gain envelopes; JS request time is not audible onset. Prioritize spoken ultimates and weapon impacts, cull lower priority tails or chatter, and measure the output. Concurrency ducking needs explicit attack/release and recovery. The GDC mix abstract supports iterative content/technology work; its full talk was not watched. [S5-WEBAUDIO,S5-CONCURRENCY,S5-GDC-MIX]
