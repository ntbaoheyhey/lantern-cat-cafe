# S11 healer audio routing

Prepared before product edits on 16 November 2026. Scope: fixed-timing healer sound semantics, without changing combat/visual clocks, damage, economy, ART presentation, voices, music or admission limits. Ranged launch/travel and all-event physical onset remain separate gaps.

Sources:

1. [W3C Web Audio 1.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/webaudio/#AudioBufferSourceNode), formal Working Draft 22 September 2026: source starts and buffer offsets have audio-clock semantics; playback rate changes delivered duration. Keep actual start observations separate from perceived or physical onset.
2. [MDN source start](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/AudioBufferSourceNode/start): offsets/duration refer to the original buffer; do not extend or move the contact beat to accommodate a sound.
3. [MDN playbackRate](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/AudioBufferSourceNode/playbackRate): a changed playback rate resamples the buffer and changes duration. Preserve the existing small pitch range and account for it in duration measurements.
4. [MDN AudioParam ramps](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/AudioParam/linearRampToValueAtTime): gain changes belong on the audio clock. Preserve the engine's existing source envelopes and music ducking; no global gain change is justified by this routing bug.
5. Local REQUEST-SHIP.txt lines 138/157: a healer requires a soft rising chime, never an impact sound. A contact event role is not itself evidence that its samples meet that requirement.
6. Local catalog.ts, audio-timeline.ts and sequence.ts: Clover, Cinnamon, Marigold and Teacup Healer have combatClass=healer. Fixed timings stay basic 350ms contact/800ms total and ultimate 1100ms contact/2200ms total.
7. Local cues.ts resolveDesign and public/audio/manifest.json: ult requests resolve their element-specific kit ahead of their generic binding. Existing healer charges and tails contain impact-sounds layers. heal.medium exists through designs/bindings; its prior six maximize variants plus click/tick layer are not automatically a verified rising chime.
8. Local impact-check-1.json: fresh production12 journeys have 248/248 causal contacts and 36 verified receipts, but Clover emits 24 basic and eight ultimate garden impacts. Onset estimates peak at 101.186/96.670ms and remain failing independently of semantics.

Measured preimplementation comparison: healer-routing-comparison-2.json evaluates four healer owners, basic/ultimate and full/short/off modes (24 cases). Current routing contains impact samples in 24/24 cases. Replacing only the contact leaves impact samples in 12/24 and weapon movement in 12/24. Class-aware routing, using a non-impact magic charge, one healing contact and no weapon movement/tail, has zero impact-sample cases and preserves contact timing and voice in 24/24. Comparison1 incorrectly inspected bindings without the element override; it and its exact harness are retained. Comparison2 uses the real production resolver and explicitly checks Clover's override.

Published-sample diagnostic: healer-sample-analysis-1.json analyzes all six unnormalized Opus maximize slices using 20ms Hann FFT windows and 10ms hops. Variants001/003/005/006 have positive dominant-peak slopes (10.092/15.613/3.778/1.523Hz per ms); variants002/004 do not. This is not a perception or fundamental-pitch proof. Use the four increasing variants for heal.medium, remove its click/tick sweetener, keep the existing transient envelope/gain and pitch range, and give the contact its existing combat-impact admission priority60. No generated audio or new packs are needed.

Decision: route fixed-timing healer basics to one heal.medium at the original contact; keep ultimate voice at0 and use kit.magic.charge at600 followed by the same healing contact. Remove the weapon whoosh and impact-bearing tail for this class. Test all healer owners and all cinematic settings, check resolved sample families, unchanged non-healer tracks, and unique contact playback through BattleAudio. Then run required regression and fresh production/fixture audio measurements, including 30-minute endurance. Human softness/intelligibility and physical onset remain unverified; do not lower any gate or claim global weapon coverage.
