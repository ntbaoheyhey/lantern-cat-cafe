## How professionals approach this

- Prefer activated protocol specifications and current releases over old pitch claims.
- Separate block interval, consensus finality, RPC response latency and receipt availability.
- Read all deployed addresses at one fixed block; bytecode presence alone is not source verification.
- Show receipt gasUsed multiplied by effectiveGasPrice; never hardcode a dollar fee.
- Keep novel L1/VRF work out of the frozen contract scope.
- Compare organizer pages and preserve the earlier deadline when contradictory.
- Scan the current tree and history separately; never persist raw secret matches.
- Validate a proposed optimization with measurements before applying it.
