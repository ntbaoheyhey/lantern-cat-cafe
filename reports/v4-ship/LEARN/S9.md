## Measured comparison

The reproducible `tools/v4ship-lab/research/model.mjs` compares two revenue mixes at the same10,000 monthly active users and250 distinct payers. Pass-led:200 passes at$7 plus100 cosmetic buyers at$8, with50 buyers in both groups, gives$2,200/month and$8.80 ARPPU. Cosmetics-led:100 passes plus200 cosmetic buyers with the same50-person overlap gives$2,300 and$9.20 ARPPU. Difference$100, or4.545%. These are calculations under assumptions, not observed demand; they do not establish a winner.

Separate low/base/high plans produce$70/$2,200/$30,000 recurring gross. Market treasury remains150/1,500/7,500YARN; it is never added to USD. Genesis one-time receipts remain separate from recurring revenue. A source-config hash is not a deployed-config attestation.

The extractor independently checks both200-seed original boundary ledgers. Mean YARN conservation residuals are below6e-10 and Star balances conserve; sink totals match burn. Median ratios are preserved independently, because ratios of means are not median ratios. Full E3 remains16/18; E4/E5 and class baseline remain unqualified.

## Application and limits

Use benchmark distributions as context with their dates and populations. Lifetime conversion cannot be used as monthly conversion; source case studies do not establish our pass conversion, cosmetic share or game profitability. Current Fuji demo cash revenue is zero. Infrastructure, acquisition, tax, refunds and customer support are outside gross receipts.

The official legal history reveals a2026amendment. Inspection of116/2026ChapterVI did not identify anArticle57 repeal; Form57 repeal is unrelated. Keep the Vietnam launch legal flag, no cash-out promise and no assertion that testnet status alone establishes compliance.

Deliver revenue assumptions and equations, the conserved economy flow with source hashes, and a six-criterion judging map. Keep hosted-config equality and missing film/live acceptance visibly open.
