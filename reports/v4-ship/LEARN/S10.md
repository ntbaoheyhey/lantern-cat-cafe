## Sources and decisions

- [WCAG2.2 §1.2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#captions-prerecorded), formal: prerecorded audio needs synchronized captions. Include meaningful voice lines and sound cues, not only promotional labels. A complete accessibility claim requires broader review.
- [FFmpeg encoding options](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.html#Main-options): two-pass encoding uses first-pass statistics to target bitrate. A hard delivery size needs a bitrate budget and a final measured byte count.
- [FFmpeg filters](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#subtitles-1): libass burns captions into frames; SSIM compares aligned video frames. Verify that the local binary includes libass. SSIM alone does not establish readable text.
- [Playwright video recording](https://playwright.dev/docs/videos): a manually created context must close before its video is saved. For this project, reuse the existing compositor-timestamp method and real WebAudio tee to retain audio/video timing; preserve raw evidence outside reports.
- [Vite public base path](https://vite.dev/guide/build.html#public-base-path): build-time imported/CSS/HTML assets follow the configured base; dynamically assembled URLs need explicit handling. A successful server build does not prove file-based offline operation.
- [MDN service workers](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Service_Worker_API/Using_Service_Workers): registration needs a secure origin, with localhost supported. A worker is not a first-open `file:` solution. Prefer the repository's existing packed-asset offline loader, with an explicit mock-chain adapter and permanent label.
- Local implementations: `scripts/v3feel/capture.mjs` (read-only method reference), `scripts/audio/capture-page.mjs` (unaltered audio tee), `tools/cardrpg-a6/build.mjs` and `tools/v3-demo/offline-public.js` (packed assets). These references do not authorize editing ART files or shipping historical footage as V4.
- [Compression experiment](../S10/codec-comparison-1.json): an identical12-second historical real-build segment, 1280×720/30fps, encoded using two methods. Raw intermediates stay in `work/v4ship-prep/S10-codec-1/`.
