# Fresh-eyes self-review

Five separate review passes by the same primary agent. These are self-grades, not independent judges or a human playtest. Images come from the running build. The retained prior report gives effects 7.8, judge/art 7.9 and overall 7.95.

## Pass 1 · first-time player
8.4/10. Loadout, slots, set hints and three live previews explain the preparation loop. Current and next numbered round make the first decision legible. Intent/skill/consumable controls remain keyboard accessible. The party footer can require scrolling at 720px; the under-two-second reading goal is not externally measured.

## Pass 2 · skeptical three-minute judge
7.9 → 8.4/10. Larger faces, warm wood/cloth, stitched and ceramic enemies and visible equipment are clear upgrades in the paired Shield Bash frames. The boss is detailed but remains a friendly toy; menace is modest.

## Pass 3 · active player
8.5/10. Chaining, neighbor shields, burn duration, cleanse, charge, stagger and sets have rule tests. 17/30 Auto versus 23/30 or 26/30 active-proxy wins supports meaningful decisions, not a claim of human skill. The browser proof chooses the first loot and loses at the boss: a useful nontrivial failure, retained.

## Pass 4 · effects director
7.8 → 8.3/10. Review covers all 43 impact frames and representative five-beat strips. The quiet bottom third has expanded grounded signatures and side-offset emblems in vfx.ts. Element paths, per-recipe glyphs, branching storm and three portrait ultimates differ. Shared rendering machinery still produces family resemblance. The absolute “none generic” gate is not independently established.
