# A10 — landing illustration, thumbnail and class icons
Question: show the real four-cat identity and classes in promotional assets that remain readable when small.
Method before build: use a calm text area and a single hierarchy of character faces. Compare an evenly spaced lineup against an asymmetric, larger grouping on own art. Use an original painted café setting and preserve costumes/props/face shapes from approved cats. Thumbnail gets its own 16:9 composition. Icons need visibly different silhouettes at32px; shape carries class along with colour. Generated promotional illustration must not be labelled as gameplay footage. SHIP integrates and adds live typography.
Sources:
- A10-saliency [paper] https://ilab.usc.edu/publications/doc/Itti_etal98pami.pdf — multiscale local intensity/colour/orientation contrast attracts attention; practical inference is to keep face regions distinct from background detail.
- A10-steam [doc] https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/standard — key art communicates gameplay, logos must remain readable small; quiet backgrounds avoid competition. We borrow composition advice, not Steam-specific delivery sizes.
- A10-youtube [doc] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en —16:9 JPG/PNG thumbnails; current guide recommends3840x2160, min640width. Target high-resolution original plus1280x720web delivery under2MB.
- A10-contrast [doc] https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html — meaningful graphical cues need3:1 adjacency contrast; thin antialiased lines can reduce readability. Measure chosen outline against the intended UI backgrounds, do not claim global conformance from an image alone.
