# A5 — Clover ally healing poses and painted leaves

Preparatory study during A4 validation, 16 November. No A5 production adoption yet.

Question: how can the same small healer make each heal recipient obvious without hostile shapes, hit poses, shake or a green flood?
Method from the sources: use a distinct cast silhouette and a clearly located emission point; let small cream/sage leaves rise at the actual healed ally. Keep the main ultimate behind the actors, with open negative space around faces. Each recipient activates exactly at its scheduled heal event. Use drawn K1 gathering the cup, K2 opening the forward paw toward allies, K4 a tucked recoiling pose, K5 lifting the cup with the free arm extended. Clover stays planted. The same hard swaps and face/foot-guarded smear recipe from A1 apply. Healing K2 means release, not a physical strike.
