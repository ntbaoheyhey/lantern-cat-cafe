3. FILM REVIEW PROTOCOL ("Codex must re-watch the film")
---------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Record the 3 fixtures (wave1, wave3, boss) at x1, 1280x720, 30 fps, with audio, 30 s each, into reports/v4-art/REVIEW/<item>/{wave1,wave3,boss}.webm with scripts/v3feel/capture.mjs as it is. One 1440x900 boss film per item that changes layout.
3.2 Watch every action window of the item's scope at 10 fps (0.3 s before to 0.5 s after impact) on contact sheets; look at every sheet of your scope. For pose and effect items also build the same beat from the reference at the same scale into a private side-by-side sheet (ref/ folder, gitignored).
3.3 REVIEW/<item>/REVIEW.md: per fixture a table of actions (time, actor, basic/ult, which pose is on screen at anticipation / contact / recovery, effect shape, target reaction) and the defect checklist (yes/no + frame): camera moved? unit facing away? units uneven? attacker silhouette unchanged during its attack (hero set: FAIL)? effect a blob/ring/fog? ultimate covering > 45% of the field? pasted-PNG look? faces washed out? number clutter? Then a 10-line honest critique vs the reference.
3.4 SCOPE RULE: each defect is tagged with its owner item. A "yes" owned by the current item (or a done item = regression) is a FAIL; a "yes" owned by a later item is logged in that item's QUEUE row and does NOT block the current one. Non-hero units never block hero items.
3.5 Timing truth: beat timing and overlaps come from the deterministic presentation schedule; the film confirms within +/- 1 frame (33 ms). Capture jitter below 1 frame is not a defect. Do not chase recorder jitter (the old Q1 50 ms residual is closed as "recorder, not game").

4. METHOD
---------
4.1 APPRENTICE METHOD (F14): STUDY (LEARN file) -> DRILL (try the move on our own art in tools/v3craft-lab/, 2+ variants) -> COMPARE with the master at the same scale (private side-by-side) -> PRODUCE (only moves that passed their drill go into the game).
4.2 HYBRID POSE SYSTEM (the core of this order):
  - K0 idle = the designer's idle loop when delivered (A8), otherwise the current mesh-breathing painting.
  - K1 windup, K2 strike, K4 hit, K5 cast = your drawn key poses (cats: all four; enemies: K1, K2, K4; boss: K1, K2, K4, K5). K3 recover and K6 ko optional.
  - HARD swaps on the presentation schedule, no cross-fades: basic = K1 from 0.05 s, hold to ~0.27 s, smear 0.27-0.33 s, K2 from 0.33 s, impact 0.35 s with 60-90 ms hit-stop, hold K2 to ~0.50 s, back to K0 before 0.80 s. Ultimate = focus 0-0.60 s, K1 -> K5 around 0.30 s, cast 0.60-1.10 s, impact 1.10 s, K5 -> K0 before 2.20 s. Target = K4 for 120-200 ms, 1-2 frame white flash, recoil 6-12 px and return.
  - Seams are hidden by: the swap landing on an impact or smear frame, a 1-2 frame smear (stretched copy or motion-blur pass of the new pose), the flash, and the effect launched from the pose's socket (hand/weaponTip). Facing: cats 3/4 right, enemies 3/4 left.
  - Every pose: same canvas and baseline as K0, real alpha, nothing cropped, sockets (feet, hand, weaponTip, faceBox) in poses/manifest.json. Reuse the runtime in vfx-craft/drawn-poses.ts; generalise it, do not fork it per unit.
4.2b CLASS MOTION AND EFFECT LANGUAGE (V4; class per unit comes from the rules field written by SHIP item S2b; until it lands, use: front-row guardian/striker = MELEE, rear witch/hexer/controller = RANGED, healer = HEALER; enemies: guard/charger/elite/flanker/boss = MELEE, ranger = RANGED, healer = HEALER; demo team = Mochi melee, Panko melee, Soot ranged, Clover healer):
  - MELEE: K1 crouch/wind-up in place, dash with a 1-2 frame smear and a dust kick, K2 strike at the target's front edge with a weapon arc (painted, own texture) and contact sparks, stronger hit-stop (80-100 ms), K4 + push-back on the target, dash back to the slot; the attacker never overlaps the target by more than 15%; other units do not move.
  - RANGED: K1 aim/raise, launch flash at the socket, a shaped projectile or beam (travel <= 0.30 s, trail, colour of the unit's flavour), impact burst on the target, K2 recoil in place.
  - HEALER: K5/K1 cast pose toward allies, effects ONLY on allies: soft rising light/leaf/steam shapes, a ring of light at the feet is allowed only as a small accent, green "+N" numbers, the healed ally gets a brief brighten (no K4 hit pose, no shake, no damage colours). A healer never shows a damage-style shape on an enemy.
  - Each class has a consistent shape language and colour rule across units, written in LEARN/A1b.md and applied to every hero unit (A3-A7). Ultimates keep the per-unit distinctness rule (G-FX) inside their class language.
4.3 TIME SPLIT: authoring (drawing, choosing, rigging, effect work) >= 50% of logged item time; measuring and logging <= 25%. Log start/end of each step in PROGRESS-ART.log; at item end write the split in STATE. If measuring passes 25%, stop adding checks and author.
4.4 POSE LADDER (climb when a pose fails its gates; never lower the bar): Rung 1 one generation from runtime.webp with a precise pose prompt (body angle, weapon position, limb positions, facing, same costume/palette/outline). Rung 2 generate from the closest accepted pose of the same unit (K1 from K0, K2 from K1). Rung 3 generate parts (arm + weapon, torso twist) and composite onto K0 with a cleaned seam. Rung 4 paint-over/correction pass on the best candidate (your own editing of OUR image: crop, warp, recolour, outline). Max 4 candidates per rung per pose.
4.5 EFFECT LADDER: Rung 1 code-drawn rings/orbs/fog as main shapes = BANNED (may stay only as small accents < 10% of the effect's area). Rung 2 shaders on our textures (erosion/dissolve, UV-pan, flow). Rung 3 offline painted flipbooks (your generated original textures, 8-16 frames, atlas <= 2048 px) driven by shaders. Rung 4 a tool proposal for the user in NEEDS-USER.md (exact commands, size, why), while you continue with Rung 3.
