## Critique against the private reference study

1. Soot now changes from a closed anticipation to a clear extended strike.
2. The enlarged silhouette makes the cloak and staff action readable.
3. The finite projectile has separate launch, travel and impact locations.
4. The reference has a sharper, more concentrated contact core.
5. Our flame contour is original and keeps the standing faces readable.
6. The long held cast is less expressive than the reference limb follow-through.
7. Cloth and tail overlap add life but do not replace another drawing.
8. The retaliation wince is readable; encoded recovery blocks reduce movie polish.
9. Remaining hero floods compete with the flame and require A4–A9.
10. Exact reference x1 tempo is uncertain, so no borrowed timing precision is claimed.
