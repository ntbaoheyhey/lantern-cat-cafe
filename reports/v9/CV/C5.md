# C5 battle mix (Claude visual lane)

`apps/web/src/audio/ultimate-mix.ts` (rules) + small hooks in `apps/web/src/audio/engine.ts`.

- Ultimate duck: on the director's `cutin` beat the music bed (new `ultDuck` node, music path only) drops 6 dB in 50 ms, holds 1.5 s, recovers in 0.4 s. Independent of the existing voice duck.
- AGS S2 layers: `public/audio/v9/sfx-ult-<cat>-<riser|transient|body|tail>.opus` (m4a fallback) load when battle or boss music starts and play on the beats: cutin -> riser, impact -> transient + body, linger -> tail. Once all four layers of a cat load, its legacy `ult.<cat>.*` single-file cues are skipped (recorded as `v9-layers`). Voice lines are unchanged.
- Normal hits (`hit.*`): +/-5% playback-rate spread per hit group.
- At most 3 copies of the same sfx id: the oldest groups are stopped first.
- Master limiter already existed (DynamicsCompressor -4 dB, 20:1, then -2.25 dB headroom); kept as is.
- Checked in the real build (muted output): engine log shows `sfx.ult.{mochi,soot,clover}.{riser,transient,body,tail}: played` and `ult.<cat>.charge|tail: v9-layers` for all three ultimates. Not listened to by ear.
- Tests: `ultimate-mix.test.ts` + all `src/audio` suites, 97/97; `tsc --noEmit` clean.
- Depends on AGS's files merging from `ag/v9-sound`; without them the legacy cues keep playing.
