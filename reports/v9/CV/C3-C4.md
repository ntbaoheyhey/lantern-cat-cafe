# C3 five-beat ultimate framework + C4 three ultimates (Claude visual lane)

Code: `apps/web/src/game/cardrpg/ultimate/` (timeline, recipes, painter, flipbooks, director) wired into `cardrpg/spine/scene.ts`.

- Runs inside the existing 2200 ms ultimate clock (impact 1100), so rules, pacing and audio timelines are unchanged.
- Beats: dim to ~40% at 0 ms (held to 1600, off by 2000); cut-in 0-650 ms (head-and-shoulders crop of `designer/cutin.png`, E7 rune circle, speed lines, outlined gradient name); cast on the frozen idle frame (120 ms squash, lunge 3.5-9% of the field toward the target, hold, step back at 1450-1800, video resumes); impact at 1100 (E1 core, E2 rings, debris, trauma shake, 90 ms hit-stop that also freezes the beat clock, ult numbers drawn 1.55x with the shared overshoot); linger (E3 smoke + embers). Rim light and three afterimages are silhouettes of the held video frame.
- Reduced motion: no lunge, shake, speed lines or hit-stop; banner fades in place.
- Old crafted/painted recipes are skipped for these three ults only; the older banner is hidden while the V9 one shows. A painter fault is caught so it can never stop the battle loop.
- Audio hooks: `window` event `th:ult-beat` {beat, cat, cue} with cues `sfx.ult.<cat>.{cutin,cast,impact,tail}` for C5/AGS.
- C4: Mochi "Last Lantern Vow" (lantern flare, golden ward dome over the team, gold shockwave toward the enemies), Clover "Fourleaf Spring Brew" (tea spiral into a four-leaf clover, healing rain and glow on allies), Soot "Chimney Comet" (comet arc onto the target, fire dome, flying coal, bursts on every enemy). Names updated in `squad/turn3d/card-effect-data.ts`.
- CXV flipbooks (`/vfx/v9/<id>.webp+json`) replace the procedural core, ring, smoke, dome, clover, comet and fire dome automatically when present.
- All drawing is original procedural canvas work (tool: hand-written Canvas2D code, no generator, no reference input). OMG 3Q frames 57-65 s were only viewed side by side for pacing and scale; they are not stored here.
- Tests: `ultimate/timeline.test.ts` (9) + spine, battle-v3 and stage suites, 431/431 + 163/163; `tsc --noEmit` clean.
- Known gaps: Soot still uses its old rig until the designer video lands (no frozen-frame cast or rim for Soot until then); the cut-in text can sit under a world HP badge on some seats.
