## Hosting limit interpretation
Vercel documents CLI source-upload limits of100MB on Hobby and1GB on Pro, plus15,000source files; files generated during a hosted build have a different count treatment. The prebuilt command uploads `.vercel/output`, and the documented archive option changes packaging. The local248.74MB/7,943file total therefore identifies a conditional Hobby upload concern, not a confirmed deployment failure: plan, actual prebuilt packaging, deduplication and authenticated output are unverified. Sources checked17Nov2026: [Vercel limits](https://vercel.com/docs/limits), [prebuilt deployment](https://vercel.com/docs/cli/deploy#prebuilt).
