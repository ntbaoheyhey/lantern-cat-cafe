# Taskbar Hero — 15-minute player test

Tóm tắt: chơi 15 phút từ tài khoản mới, vừa chơi vừa nói điều bạn đang nghĩ.
Đừng đọc hướng dẫn trước hoặc nhờ người điều phối chọn đội giúp.
Ghi lại chỗ không hiểu, lúc muốn dừng, và lựa chọn thật sự làm trận đấu khác đi.
Cuối buổi trả lời tám câu bên dưới; gửi câu trả lời cùng tên bản game và ảnh nếu tiện.
Các báo cáo tự động là số liệu tham khảo, chưa thay thế buổi thử với người thật này.

Use the reviewed A5 game linked by the coordinator. Record its filename and version before starting. The public `docs/cardrpg3/index.html` may still be the earlier demo; do not assume it is A5. Start a fresh save in a separate browser profile so your existing game is safe. Desktop sizes: 1280×720, 1440×900, or 1024×600.

## Play for 15 minutes

| Time | Player task | Observer notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 min | Begin the lesson. Choose where a cat stands and play the automatic fight. | Can the player find the next action? Do they understand what their placement changed? |
| 3–6 min | Open an earned invitation. Compare an affordable item, then equip it if you want. | Can they explain the cost, rarity, odds, and what the item changes without help? |
| 6–10 min | Choose a route. Read a boss clue and prepare a crew or plan. | Does the clue affect a choice? Note any long wait or confusing control. |
| 10–13 min | Play another fight and read the result. Try its suggestion if available. | Can they name a cat or item that helped, and explain their next change? Record losses too. |
| 13–15 min | Visit the journal and return Home. Decide what you would do tomorrow. | Do stars, discoveries, cafe service or tomorrow’s reward give a reason to return? |

Let the player explore. Do not point to the correct button or explain a system unless they ask; record the question before helping. Keep normal speed for the first fight. Afterwards, let the player use speed and skip naturally, and note when they do. Stop at 15 minutes even if the route is unfinished. A day-7 imported save is optional follow-up, separate from this fresh-player test.

## Eight questions

1. What did you think you should do first? What made that clear or unclear?
2. Which choice changed a fight? Name the cat, item, row or plan, and what you noticed.
3. Why did you win or lose the latest fight? What would you change next?
4. What did the item you compared give you? Was its cost worth paying?
5. Where would you check invitation odds, the guaranteed reward and the collectible supply?
6. Which moment was exciting? Which wait or screen made you want to leave?
7. Would you play tomorrow? Score 1–10 and explain what would bring you back.
8. What one change would most improve your next 15 minutes?

## Send the results back

Reply in the project chat with the game filename/version, viewport, whether you had played before, minutes played, and answers numbered 1–8. Include the first confusing screen, any unexpected loss, and the point where you stopped. Screenshots and an exported save are optional; do not include passwords, wallet information or other private data.

The observer should also record: meaningful choices made, rewards noticed, celebrations noticed, requests for help, and approximate idle waits. Keep observations separate from interpretations. One person’s response is useful feedback, not a retention rate or proof that a balance gate passes.
