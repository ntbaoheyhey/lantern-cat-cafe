# Delayed boss impact49 — before samples

Current24 isolated replays show that an ordinary HP/defense margin can buy an entire extra guardian turn. This diagnostic tests giving the guardian a bounded response window without reducing the nominal hit or changing enemy difficulty, set identity, gear budgets, RNG, targeting, or the choice policy's lookahead depth. It is a separate hypothesis from threat timing47, not a combination with that candidate.

Two fixed arms delay 25% or 50% of a boss's post-shield, post-link direct HP damage until the affected cat finishes its next own action. The rounded remainder lands immediately; delayed damage retains its source, bypasses shields already accounted for at the original hit, and is settled before checking the result of the cat's action. Actual HP loss remains capped by remaining health as in production. A cat killed by the immediate part cannot recover the deferred amount as health or incur a second defeat. Delayed amounts are included as liabilities in the existing one-action utility, never as free health. OFF, tutorials, nonboss hits and all other rule paths stay unchanged. This is automatic: no in-fight input.

Before sampling the unchanged 384 fourth-slot cells at TRAIN1–50, each arm must pass 18 full automatic replays on the same six current24 inputs (ON/OFF/tutorial), exact initial-state parity, exact 12 OFF/tutorial replays, 45 historical OFF streams, and choice immutability with outstanding damage. Controlled synthetic fixtures check rounded damage conservation, exactly one own-action settlement, original source attribution, shields earned after the hit, immediate defeat, tutorial/nonboss exclusion, clone continuity, and simultaneous final defeat. Synthetic fixtures are not earned progression evidence.
