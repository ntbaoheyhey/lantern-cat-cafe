# B1 diagnostic interpretation

Read after all 31 train cells and six read-only paired traces completed. These observations do not use acceptance seeds.

- Main stats have the largest isolated gain in 28/31 residual cells; affixes lead in 3/31. The fourth-set threshold alone is a null outcome in these residual fixtures. Do not weaken all eight set identities on the unsupported assumption that their fourth effect caused these failures.
- Hearthloom Legendary charm, Mochi, chapter 3: no piece wins 16/500, main alone 343/500, affixes alone 76/500, unique alone 16/500, threshold alone 16/500, full piece 473/500. This is a survival cliff involving ordinary item stats and interactions, not evidence that displayed utility alone should be inflated.
- Bellsteel Legendary charm, same carrier and chapter: 35/500 becomes 438/500 with its main stat alone, and 493/500 with all components. None of the six selected paired replays uses Defend before the decisive loss. A proposed Defend-loop cap cannot explain this dominant pattern.
- In all six losing examples Mochi falls on round 6. Chapter-3 examples have 278→321, 348→348, and 260→324 HP requests; the corresponding deficits to survive that hit are 44, 1, and 65 HP. Chapter-1 examples have larger deficits, 228 and 203 HP, and win after offensive gear lets the remaining cats finish sooner.
- The first changed party decision is sometimes after death, not before it. Bellsteel Rare and Hearthloom Legendary seed 1 share the same initial decisions through round 6; the winning build retains Mochi's round-7 ultimate. Do not describe every divergence as an AI decision bug.
- Source review also finds discontinuous automatic utility: a 65-point penalty below 30% HP and a heal bonus abruptly enabled below 50% HP. These may amplify a marginal stat change. Their causal contribution is still UNMEASURED; a smooth alternative needs its own paired train run.
