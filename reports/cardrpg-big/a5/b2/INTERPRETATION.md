# B2 training evidence, before tuning

The A4 selected coherent crew exceeded random equal-power triples by 47.73 pp on the old acceptance sample. This is a combined team-selection contrast: cat kits, formation and crew bonuses change together. It is not an isolated estimate of the bonus magnitude.

The existing TRAIN matrix contains 14, 31 and 61 distinct triples within five points of the best team for chapters 1, 2 and 3. Every boss has a 100% best rate. Thus the literal claim that only one team works is unsupported, but saturation makes these counts weak evidence of healthy diversity. The new elite coverage remains unmeasured. Preserve these old results when calibrating the common reference fixture.

The registered same-cats ablation holds original normalized stats and gear fixed, removing only crew stat/opening bonuses. All 48 baseline cells reproduce original TRAIN outcomes. Its deliberately stratified mean is +8.458 pp, with -8 to +30 pp across cells; this is not a representative population mean. Four cells reach at least 24 pp, and negative changes are retained. A blanket threshold reduction is not justified by the selected-team gap alone.

Brioche's Apron Link currently redirects part of HP damage straight into the protector's HP via loseHp, bypassing any remaining shield. This is a source-level observation, not proof of how many defeats it causes. The registered link probe uses four pre-existing Brioche triples, three bosses and TRAIN seeds 1–50. It records shared hits with stored shields and local lethal margins, verifies baseline outcomes and read-only observer parity, and does not change rules. Its local preventable-HP bound must not be reported as a counterfactual win-rate gain.

Next: inspect those traces, test a narrowly scoped shield interaction if warranted, then compare all cat kits on a consistent reference fixture. Retain old/new fixture numbers and freeze calibration before any fresh acceptance sample. Fresh seeds 20001–20500 remain unused.
