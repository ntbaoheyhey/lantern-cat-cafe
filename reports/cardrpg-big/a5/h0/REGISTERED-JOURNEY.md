# H0 baseline protocol

Baseline artifact: A4 P7 SHA-256 `067d2062f6836a6d0bb9f6753ece1b2f3ca923688f241866a7f42806c8f7b6a1`.
One fresh save, one persistent headless Chromium page, 1280×720, sixty active elapsed minutes. Battles play at x1, with no skip or clock acceleration. Between shared-lock leases the page remains open at a safe UI boundary; handoffs are excluded from active time and preserved as wall timestamps. Each lease ends after a safe action, at about ten minutes and under twenty minutes.

The deterministic policy follows practice, welcome invitation, available equipment/leveling, Chapter1 routes, affordable claims/draws and periodic journal visits. It chooses the first route/reward/affordable event, heals at camps, saves points at shops. The first campaign changes Care to Protect. It never injects currency, levels, unlocks, or gameplay commands into the browser. Reading allowances are 3.5 seconds before decisions and five seconds on results; this is an automation assumption, not measured human reading behavior.

Metrics at 5/30/60 active minutes: reward receipts (positive balances/new gear/new cats); visible reveal ceremonies; offered preparation/equipment choices whose options produce distinct deterministic outcomes on train seeds1/2/3; noninteractive battle seconds; other waiting seconds; words per visited screen; visible developer controls and seed labels. Report repeated offers and unique contexts separately. A different HP/action-count outcome qualifies, but is distinct from changing victory probability. Route choices remain unverified until paired outcomes exist; do not count navigation or speed controls as meaningful choices.

Dead time is reported conservatively: x1 combat time offers no gameplay decision or committed reward and is counted as noninteractive time, even when animation is enjoyable. Reading time on a decision screen is reported separately. Do not relabel these assumptions after seeing the A5 result. The same policy and metric definitions must be reused or any adaptation disclosed.

Day7 reproduction is a separate real-click x1 journey using the unchanged public-command-earned P6 day7 save (SHA62b7006757b7c325ea0bc6e7d0ad20fe358caafc4568b539525d42e0b3e0efac), matching the A4 first-node/reward/event path. Record complete battle logs, rows, crew/gear/plan, first death and damage attribution. An import reproduces an earned fixture; it is not a claim that this browser played seven elapsed days.

No new acceptance seed from20001–20500 or30001–30500 is used by H0. These metrics are proxies, not a human playtest. Keep original failed harness receipts.

## Harness correction before the valid sample
Attempt 1 stopped after detecting a policy loop: the script required equipping an unowned spare item before entering a route, but all six starter items were already equipped. It revisited menus without advancing. Its complete available receipts are retained under `session-attempt-1/` and are excluded from baseline metrics. The corrected script visits equipment once, then continues to earn gear through routes; it does not alter the game. Visible word counts now exclude unselected dropdown options and collapsed text. Both corrections precede the valid sample and apply unchanged to AFTER measurements.
