# A3 automatic encounter presentation
REFERENCE: apps/web/src/game/cardrpg/depth/model.ts, Battle.tsx, Encounter.tsx, Pages.tsx, RunMap.tsx

Prepare shows the three companions, their rows and equipped items, opponent power and five alternate-seed simulations. Its Easy / Even / Hard estimate names the sample size. Fight begins automatic playback. The team screen derives unlocked kits from progression.

Battle exposes speed, pause, skip, camera, sound, log and leave. The result screen shows victory or defeat, contribution-based MVP, per-cat actual damage/healing/damage-taken, the first ultimate, largest hit and saves when present. Recorded replay keeps the original seed and choices. Improve team settles the encounter and ends an unfinished route before opening team building, with that consequence disclosed on screen.

Screenshot review moved Fight and result actions before the detail panels to put them in the first viewport. The first path harness used an aria-hidden icon in a button name and timed out. The corrected accessible-name selector passes the complete paths.
