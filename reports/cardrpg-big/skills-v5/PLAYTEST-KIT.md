# Skills V5 — fifteen minutes at The Lantern

Bản thử này có Burn, Bleed, Bitter và combo; trận đấu vẫn tự động hoàn toàn.
Chơi bằng bản offline Skills V5 riêng, không dùng bản A4 ở trang index.
Các phép đo bằng bot không thay thế cảm nhận của người chơi.
Sau hai trận, thử gọi tên trạng thái trên một nhân vật mà không mở hướng dẫn.
Gửi tám câu trả lời ngắn bên dưới, kèm thời điểm hoặc ảnh nếu có chỗ khó hiểu.

Open [Skills V5 demo](../../../docs/cardrpg3/skills-v5.html) locally on a desktop. Use 1280×720 or 1440×900; 1024×600 is also supported. Keep playback at 1× for this check. No wallet or sign-in is needed.

|Minutes|Try this|Watch for|
|---|---|---|
|0–3|Start the practice table. Place Soot in front, then put Mochi back in front. Fight and open the earned invitation.|Can you predict who takes the first hit? Is the next step obvious?|
|3–6|Choose the first upgrade, inspect its comparison, then equip it and start the route.|Does the comparison explain a consequence you care about?|
|6–10|Play two automatic encounters. Read the plain status strip; hover or focus a keyword in the glossary. Read the Result combo message.|Can you name the affected cat, the status and what it does? Do the numbers match the explanation?|
|10–13|Open Companions. Read one shape and combo role. If you own two partners, place them together.|Does the crew hint help you choose? No unlocked crew is required to finish the test.|
|13–15|Review tonight’s goal, the next reward and the Field guide. Export your save in Save & settings.|Would you know what to try tomorrow?|

Please answer these eight questions:

1. After two fights, name the statuses shown on five units. Include the display name and what each does. “Unsure” is useful.
2. Which attack hit more than one foe? Did its target list make the difference clear?
3. Did you see Crumb Burst, Wildfire, Rupture or Spread? Which cat caused it, and why?
4. What did your first equipment change do? Was that visible in the next result?
5. After a loss, what specific change did the game offer? Was it available to you?
6. Where did you wait too long, lose track of the next step, or struggle to read?
7. Rate 1–10: would you return tomorrow, did choices matter, did you understand the outcome, and was anything exciting? Give one reason for each.
8. What is the single change you most want before playing again?

Return the viewport, approximate minutes played, answers 1–8, and any optional save code or screenshot. The recognition target is at least 4/5 correctly named unit states; until real answers arrive it is **NOT VERIFIED**. Keep unclear or missed answers in the results.

This lane A demo uses the inherited battle renderer and a plain status strip. Lane B’s new badges, body effects and reaction callouts are a separate draft and have not been integrated here. Judge this artifact as shown.
