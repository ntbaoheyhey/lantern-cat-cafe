The generic gate requires at least four near-best triples per encounter. The named-crew gate requires each approved crew within 5pp. A saturated encounter can satisfy both without demonstrating distinct strengths; all such ties and fixtures remain in the registered result. These elite measurements do not erase the [boss crew misses](BOSS-CREW-REVIEW.md).

Equal displayed power is a controlled budget, not a guarantee of equal challenge. Future TRAIN work should inspect encounter pressure before interpreting saturation as successful diversity. No encounter or weight has been replaced in this acceptance; any retuned candidate needs registered 30001–30500.
