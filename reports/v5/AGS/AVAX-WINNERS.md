## Key Findings (English + Chinese)

1. **On-chain Proof (链上证明):** Winning projects consistently feature a dedicated section (or even a top-level link) for on-chain verification, making it easy for judges to trust the implementation.
2. **README Structure (README 结构):** High-quality READMEs follow a "problem-solution" flow and provide extremely clear "run locally" instructions.
3. **Video Presentation (视频展示):** Projects with a concise, well-structured video (e.g., < 3 mins) that clearly demonstrates the "on-chain" aspect usually win higher scores.

## Recommendations for Taskbar Hero
*   Move the "On-chain" proof section to a more prominent position.
*   Add a specific "Problem & Solution" section at the top.
*   Ensure the demo video explicitly highlights the "on-chain" interaction in the first 30 seconds.
