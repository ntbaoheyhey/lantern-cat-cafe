## Detailed Findings

1. **On-chain Proof (链上证明):** Winning projects consistently feature a dedicated section for on-chain verification, making it easy for judges to trust the implementation. Hashfire and The Indexing Company are notable for their transparent, enterprise-grade on-chain proof presentation.
2. **README Structure (README 结构):** Top-tier READMEs follow a "problem-solution" flow, provide clear run-local instructions, and document contract addresses clearly.
3. **Video Presentation (视频展示):** Projects with a concise, well-structured video (e.g., < 3 mins) that clearly demonstrates the "on-chain" aspect usually win higher scores.

## Recommendations for Future Projects
*   Move the "On-chain" proof section to a more prominent position.
*   Add a specific "Problem & Solution" section at the top.
*   Ensure the demo video explicitly highlights the "on-chain" interaction in the first 30 seconds.
