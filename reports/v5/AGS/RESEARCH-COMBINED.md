### Key Findings
* **Performance Budgets:** E-commerce/SaaS (FCP <1.2s, LCP <2.0s) vs. Media/Rich Web Apps (FCP <1.5s, LCP <2.5s).
* **Vite/Cache:** Granular vendor chunking, hashed assets with immutable headers (Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable), index.html must be no-cache.
* **Media:** Serve AVIF/WebP, use explicit width/height for layout stability, lazy load images below the fold, lazy-initialize audio post-interaction.
* **Differentiation:** Proposed settings (e.g., target budgets) vs. measured/shipped numbers (found via Lighthouse/WebPageTest tools).
