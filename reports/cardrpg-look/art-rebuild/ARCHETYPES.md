# Shared real3D archetypes

One cat skeleton plus five foe skeletons; same scale registry as SCALE-STANDARD.md.

- **cat**: mochi, soot, clover, panko, mallow, sorrel, biscuit, inkcap, cinnamon, brioche, velvet, marigold
- **construct**: button-guard, teacup-healer, kettle-knight, crumb-sentinel, pantry-crown
- **soft-biped**: sock-skipper, binding-thread, practice-cushion
- **winged**: needle-moth, pollen-scout
- **quadruped**: spool-ram, rain-spool
- **yarn-giant**: yarn-giant

Pantry Crown uses the construct skeleton at the boss scale; Rain Spool shares quadruped motions with Spool Ram at its independent boss scale. Shared clips are idle,run-in,basic,signature,ultimate,hit,KO,victory. Outfit/prop bones stay secondary and never set body height.
