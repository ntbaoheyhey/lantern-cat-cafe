# Enemy concept source review — 2026-11-10

All ten regular foes and three bosses now have original concept masters. Button Guard and Yarn Giant are unchanged. Eleven new concepts use the built-in image tool, original prompts and no reference inputs. Receipts contain each full prompt, source hash, cleanup and review. This is source review only; it does not accept runtime geometry or animation.

Viewed enemies-concepts.webp on a neutral dark teal background. Warm upper-left key, dark red/plum values, brass details and glowing eyes are consistent. Round spool, square cushion, narrow bobbin, triangular moth, broad kettle and tall cabinet silhouettes remain distinct. No additional limbs/eyes or detached props observed. Alpha compositing confirms that the apparent haze in raw spool previews is outside the retained alpha silhouette.

Five production risks:
1. Crumb/Kettle brass is brighter than Button/Yarn; preserve dark body groups and avoid flat-pink overrides.
2. Needle/Pollen wings need independent pivots and deliberate opening beats; full-body wobble alone is insufficient.
3. Spool/Rain use four feet and low bodies; a generic upright biped partition is unsuitable.
4. Pantry crown jars are integrated into the model; VFX must not conjure duplicate jars near the hand or target.
5. Teacup rim, narrow moth legs and sewing needles need mip/edge review at real battle size.

The first three are explicit runtime configuration/review checks. Existing fallback motion and face evidence covers Button/Yarn only. Generated source quality is not a performance, parity or battle readability gate.
