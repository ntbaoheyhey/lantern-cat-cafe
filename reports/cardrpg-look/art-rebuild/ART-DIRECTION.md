# Premium cozy — art direction

Original night-cafe heroes; warmth, tactile materials and readable expressions lead.
This is a production target, not a claim about the current art.

## Shared visual language
- Friendly shapes: round cheeks, tapered paws, soft ears, one asymmetric role prop.
- Hostile shapes: broken ellipses, pointed seams, crooked handles, lit narrow eyes.
- Ink #172D36, night #102129, teal #233E46, cream #FFF0D2, honey #F3BB61.
- Mint #9CCBB1, iris #B9A5DC, coral #E78570 are restrained identity accents.
- Enemies concentrate saturated plum/crimson/ink; separate by rim and value grouping.
- Three broad light tones, soft contact AO; key upper left, cool rear-right fill.
- Detail clusters at face/prop; broad quiet fabric areas keep silhouettes readable.

## Cats
- Head 42–45% of total body height; wide cheek plane and small tapered muzzle.
- Large coloured irises, dark pupils, two asymmetric highlights; no vacant dot eyes.
- Warm translucent ears/nose, cheek tufts and fine fur direction without noisy stripes.
- Idle/attack/hurt/happy expressions use brows, eyelids, mouth and ear angle together.
- Mochi: cream lantern guardian; teal apron, brass button, rounded door shield.
- Soot: charcoal midnight hexer; folded violet hood, amber eyes, hanging crumb lantern.
- Clover: pale greenhouse healer; leaf collar, mint apron, curved glass tea vessel.
- Panko: flour baker; rolling-pin diagonal, cream cap, toasted leather mitts.
- Mallow: warm oven keeper; rounded oven mitts, coral scarf and ember whisk.
- Cinnamon: spice steward; tall spiral tuft, caramel cloth and crescent cloche.
- Velvet: elegant thread witch; sweeping cape, needle wand, long ribbon silhouette.
- Biscuit: sturdy cookie artisan; broad baker cap, biscuit shield and square apron hem.
- Brioche: braided bread host; braided crown, looped basket and soft puff sleeves.
- Marigold: floral tea tender; petal ruff, narrow kettle and sunny golden ears.
- Sorrel: herb forager; asymmetric leaf hat, long satchel and slender staff.
- Inkcap: mushroom scholar; broad dark cap, short cloak, luminous spore charm.
- Outfit/material details follow existing role assets and kit; no gameplay changes.

## Enchanted opponents
- Button Guard: polished plum ceramic button shell, brass stitches, fierce glowing eyes.
- Yarn Giant: massive wine/ink yarn coils, torn crown, luminous seams and hooked fists.
- Other regulars retain their existing object identity; no recoloured generic bodies.
- Kettle Knight: dark enamel/brass, cracked ember furnace, steam crown.
- Pantry Crown: crooked lacquer cupboard, jar jewels, warm cracks and crown aura.
- Boss projected height at least twice tallest regular; every head/foot inside safe stage.

## Animation and rendering
- Breathing, blink, ear/tail follow-through; attacks anticipate, lunge, settle.
- Hurt compresses briefly; victory opens posture; defeat slumps without anchor drift.
- Preserve named rig/clip targets, event times and protected-face anchors.
- Compare rebuilt 3D with illustrated 5–8-layer animation on identical four-unit shots.
- Choose by observed appeal/clarity/motion/performance/cost; ties favour roster completion.
- New art only under lookV2 and lookArt=v3; v2 stays available for comparison.

## Effects and composition
- Luminous core, soft volume, travelling particles, tapering ribbon, grounded light.
- One unmistakable ultimate motif per cat, 25–40% of page height at peak.
- Protected faces, badges, numbers and reaction words remain visually clear.
- Cats at least 18% of 720px viewport; regular foes at least 16%.
- Warm lamps frame a close battle; background steam/dust stays subordinate.
- No bloom or depth blur on DOM text; no animation flashes under reduced motion.

## Review discipline
- Same poster set, equal-scale lineups, actual battle peaks and continuous clip each round.
- Review silhouette, value grouping, harmony, focal point, edge control and consistency.
- Name five visible flaws by region; repair worst three, inspect again, retain misses.
- Never promote numeric readability or layered effect counts into a beauty claim.
- Generated art uses only the built-in image tool and our original inputs; record full provenance.
