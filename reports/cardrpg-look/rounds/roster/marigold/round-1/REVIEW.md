# First Marigold review

Personally viewed before/R1 front/side/back. Warm fur, green coat and rounded flask read clearly. Five flaws: collar petals form a thick necklace that touches the chin (190–295,425–455); head flower reads as a small gold knot (175–210,320–355); collar projects forward in side view; pocket is a solid sewn relief; light strap can compete with the spoon. R2 reduces and lowers collar petals, enlarges flower petals and adds a pale flower center. Pocket/strap remain declared limits and browser motion still needs review.
