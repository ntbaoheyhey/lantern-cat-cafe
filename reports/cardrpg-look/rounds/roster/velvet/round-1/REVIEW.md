# First Velvet review

Personally viewed before/R1 front/side/back. Face and purple cloth have better separation than the inherited model. Five issues: nightcap has a wedge/cut silhouette (190–290,285–350); pale brim cord appears partly detached (180–305,350–365); bell tool merges into coat (320–345,450–520); tassel is tiny from front; rear hem remains angular. R2 replaces the cap crown with a rounded dome and narrower curled end, fits the brim and lightens the bell glaze. Moving tool clearance remains untested.
