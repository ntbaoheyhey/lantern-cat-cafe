# R1 plate review

Viewed all four256×320 plates on the dark sheet. Five flaws: top rail x70–186/y8–20 is empty; four corner pins are almost identical; side rails x10–22/y35–280 have uniform value; bottom rail x40–215/y300–313 has no rarity cue; nine-pixel bevel leaves little material identity at thumbnail scale. R2 widens the enamel rail, adds original pearl/leaf/flower/sunburst top ornaments and one-to-four tactile bottom marks, and introduces restrained micro-relief and contrast. Transparent center remains required.
