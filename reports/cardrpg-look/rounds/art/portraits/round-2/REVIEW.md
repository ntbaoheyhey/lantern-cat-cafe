# R2 inherited-model portrait review

Personally inspected all twelve portraits at their 256×320 display size on ink and cream contact sheets. The nine selected inherited-model portraits are reviewed individually in REVIEW.json. The three sculpted heroes receive a separate R3 review/selection.

Closer framing, a more frontal camera and stronger soft fill improve face size and under-chin separation. These are studio improvements: the nine underlying models still show angular ears, flat aprons, repeated faces and simple accessories. They remain explicitly labelled inherited-model portraits pending L5; no sculpture completion or perfect visual acceptance is claimed. Tall hats/hooks on Brioche, Mallow and Sorrel limit their face size, but their full silhouettes remain intact.

Self-grade for the nine portraits: 6/10 overall. Sharpness 8, lighting 7, material 5. Light backgrounds expose pale ear-edge contrast; dark backgrounds expose dark coat/paw separation. The actual game card composition remains to be reviewed.
