# First crumb review

Personally viewed before/R1 front/side/back. R1 has five obvious flaws: long rectangular torso lacks biscuit identity (180–300,420–560); eye assembly floats ahead of the helmet (side 305–330,385–425); crown crust line is detached (220–290,360–375); buckler has radial shading; brown torso and hands merge in value. R2 adds a rounded biscuit torso with toasted dimples, deepens the head backing, seats the eyes and gives the buckler a smoothly rounded surface. Prior rounds retained.
