# First teacup review

Personally viewed before/R1 three angles. Real rolled lip/handle improve cup identity. Five issues: eyes float forward of cup (side 320–350,465–505); vine lines float in front of glaze at their lower ends; lip/interior has radial shading; steam begins above the liquid with a visible gap; muted gray glaze gives less ceramic contrast than the warm face colors. R2 seats eyes, lays vines onto the actual tapered cup surface and joins the steam to the tea. Radial shading gets a denser ring profile. No runtime acceptance yet.
