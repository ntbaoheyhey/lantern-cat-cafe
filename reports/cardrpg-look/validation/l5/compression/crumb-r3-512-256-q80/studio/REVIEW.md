# Packed Crumb R3 review

Personally viewed all three raw/packed studio pairs at fixed camera. Eye rims retain their attachment to the biscuit helmet; cross stitch, baton opening and tabard edge remain distinct. Compression softens fine baked pores but introduces no visible silhouette change in these pairs. The rear remains plain and the thin baton is delicate at small sizes. Minimum measured crop SSIM 0.991951; this measures fidelity, not beauty. Accepted as a packed runtime candidate, requiring a fresh runtime pose check because the earlier first-six pilot contains R2.
