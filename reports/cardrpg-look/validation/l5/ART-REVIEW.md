A skeptical three-minute judge can verify actual models, samples and size but should not infer continuous collision clearance, premium finish or final-roster performance from these screenshots.

Remaining configuration captures are machine-checked separately. This document does not claim every full-page pixel was personally inspected or that the source/timing gates have completed.
