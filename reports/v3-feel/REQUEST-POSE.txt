4B.3 POSE KIT CONTRACT (per unit; names are also the Spine animation/attachment names for designer deliveries):
  K0 idle      existing painting + breathing (keep).
  K1 windup    weight back, weapon/arm/staff drawn back or raised, body coiled; clearly different silhouette.
  K2 strike    full extension toward the target: arm/weapon thrust or swung through, body leaning or stepping in, head forward; the weapon tip/hand socket is marked for effects.
  K3 recover   optional; may reuse K2 relaxed + mesh settle back to K0.
  K4 hit       flinch: head and body snap back, eyes squeezed, ears down; used on every damaging hit received.
  K5 cast      ultimate pose: heroic, arms/weapon up or forward, cloak flared; held during focus and cast.
  K6 ko        optional; may be K4 + the existing death dissolve.
  Enemies and the boss get K1, K2, K4 and (if they have an ultimate in the fixtures) K5.
  Facing: cats 3/4 facing right, enemies 3/4 facing left; same light direction, outline weight, palette and painterly finish as K0; same canvas, scale and foot baseline as K0 (Q2 sizes must hold).
4B.4 TIMING (inside the fixed envelopes; limited animation = hold drawings, snap between them, NO cross-fades between two paintings, which ghost):
  Basic 0.80 s: 0.00 K0 -> by 0.05 K1 (ease in, slight squash), hold K1 to 0.27; 0.27-0.33 one smear (drawn smear frame or Rung-2/3 smear effect over K2), 0.33 K2 snap; 0.35 impact: attacker and target hold 60-90 ms (hit-stop) inside the beat, target swaps to K4 for 120-200 ms + 1-2 frame white flash + 6-12 px knockback and return; K2 held to about 0.50; then K3/K0 with mesh settle (overshoot <= 3%) by 0.80.
  Ultimate 2.20 s: focus 0.00-0.60 with K1-like gather then K5 snap at about 0.30 and held; cast 0.60-1.10 K5 with cloak/tail overlap; impact 1.10 (targets K4); aftermath K5 -> K0 settle by 2.20.
  Melee may hop/lunge <= 5% of width between K1 and K2 and return during recovery.

  Method B: redraw only the moving parts (arm + weapon, head tilt, cloak) with Method A on part crops of our own art, and assemble them on the existing rig as attachment swaps; body stays K0. Use when A cannot hold identity after 2 rounds for that unit.
