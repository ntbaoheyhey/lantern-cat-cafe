# Drawn action pose pipeline
Basis: LEARN/P1-poses.md; work order4B. P1 selects a method, not accepted production art. Soot must pass before rollout.

1. At item start: UTC/STOP/INBOX, designer-inbox, HANDOFF/queue, branch and current work order. Designer delivery wins. Add study sources and an own-content comparison before changing an unlearned part of this method.
2. Open the unit's approved runtime.webp/portrait.webp and accepted rig. Write costume/prop/anatomy checklist and facing. Measure K0 alpha>=128 opaque height, foot contact, head landmarks and face box; identify which image actually renders in the fixture. Do not compare generated art to a differently lit rig without recording that limitation.
3. Keep K0. Required independent drawings: K1 windup, K2 strike, K4 hit, K5 cast where ultimate exists. K3/K6 optional. Generate at least4 candidates PER required pose with built-in imagegen and ONLY approved own art inputs. One asset/candidate per call. Save exact prompt, inputs, tool, date and disposition in assets/provenance.json; raw inputs/outputs stay local and ignored until selected.
4. Prompt template: “Use case: identity-preserve. Original2D RPG <unit> <K id>, candidate<N>. Input1 approved full-body, input2 approved portrait. Same character, costume, proportions, painterly finish, upper-left light and outline. Three-quarter facing<right cats/left enemies>. <pose staging>. One full body, feet on original baseline, original apparent body/head scale, all extremities and prop inside a square canvas with margins. Transparent RGBA. No text/background/effects/extra anatomy. Keep<checklist>.” Stage K1 with weight back/weapon behind, K2 with open arm/body twist and front reach, K4 with head/body recoil and ears lowered, K5 with a strong raised/forward casting silhouette. Do not ask for a larger head to make it cute.
5. Measure all candidates before cleanup. Shared foot anchor: IoU K1<=.85,K2<=.75; K2 front reach>=.12 body height. Six seeded Lab kmeans palettes, optimal one-to-one CIEDE2000 mean<=8; head/body ratio relative.92–1.08. Keep metric failures visible. Inspect best2 per pose at2x field scale for every costume item/anatomy, light/outline, alpha and crop defects; record one-line verdict each. Low IoU alone is insufficient.
6. Normalize selected art to K0 opaque height (K1/K2 ±5%; intentional K4/K5 exception written). Clean isolated alpha/fringe, match palette carefully, export lossless WebP on the SAME640×640 canvas. Preserve the measured foot baseline; declare foot x per pose, so wider drawings can fit without moving the world foot. Check the entire prop fits with margin. Do not squeeze width independently or clip a staff to pass size. Re-run identity/silhouette metrics after cleanup.
7. Store per-pose feet, hand/weapon-tip socket, face box and opaque bounds in a shared pose manifest. Convert pixel coordinates consistently into rig local coordinates. The runtime selects one drawing at a time; hides old rig attachments while a full pose is active; restores original idle on return/death/reset. Keep mesh motion for small overlap/settle. Socket transforms follow pose scale/facing/lunge; effects never use guessed old idle offsets.
8. Basic800ms: K1 by50ms to270ms; smear270–330ms; K2 at330ms, impact350ms with60–90ms hit-stop; K2 through about500ms; settle toK0 by800ms. Every damaged target K4 for120–200ms plus1–2flash frames and6–12px knockback/return. Ultimate2200ms: gather thenK5 around300ms, focus to600ms, cast to1100ms, impact1100ms, settle by2200ms. Uniform x2/x3 scaling; serialized actions. Static camera; only permitted ultimate shake. No cross-fade, no walk-up casting.
9. Anchor a shaped basic and ultimate to K2/K5 sockets. Basic>=3layers; ultimate>=5layers and distinct travelling body. Use shader/flipbook method; no rings/fog/curtain replacement by another curtain. Soot pilot includes reworking Lantern Requiem and target hits.
10. Film the actual three fixtures at1280×720,x1,30fps,30s with audio using the existing recorder. Review every scoped attack and damaging-hit window at10fps, compare privately at the same body scale with a master beat; record frame refs and at least2 review/revision rounds. Check1440×900 too. Never accept based only on a still sheet or numeric gates.
11. Take40 random unit frames from the final films. Save blind idle/windup/strike/hit/cast labels BEFORE opening schedule labels; accuracy>=90%. Preserve labels/hash/timestamps, full pose receipts and every attack/hit window. Q1 schedule/video and Q2 size/facing/face regressions remain gates. Temporary mesh targets are explicitly OPEN until K4 exists.
12. After2 failed Method A rounds, try Method B with redrawn moving parts including torso/head/cloak as needed. The P1 arm-only experiment failed silhouette and seams. Method C remains pose OPEN. Never weaken the thresholds. Designer sets use the same names/timings/sockets.
13. Log actual active author/review/learn/measure intervals while working; no retrospective guessed time. Pose>=25author minutes, basic family>=30, ultimate>=60; author>=60%, measurement<=20%,2revisions. Provisional pose-set estimate30–45author minutes plus15–25integration and20–30review minutes is only a planning estimate from the unfinished study. P2 must calibrate it. Generation waiting alone is not drawing work.
14. Per item: typecheck, touched tests, parity and checker with completed items. Commit message cites LEARN. Keep<=12JPG sheets<=1280px and3final films<=8MB, total trackedFEEL<=300MB; raw/WAV/reference pixels never ship. Commit/push checkpoint; keep reported failures open.
