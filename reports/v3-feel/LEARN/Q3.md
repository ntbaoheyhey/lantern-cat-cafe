# Q3 apprenticeship learning

Started 2026-11-15T11:51:10.841297+00:00. This extends the existing CRAFT study. No Q3 production edits have been made. D1–D8 and the in-battle pilot are acceptance gates, not completed work.

## Question and professional method

How can our original painted layers become directional, evolving effects, while bodies and faces keep their contrast? Separate shape, motion, colour and lifetime. A mesh establishes the strike direction; panned UV detail moves through that shape. Erosion removes irregular regions of the painted alpha, so the residue breaks up instead of fading as one picture. A flipbook can preserve an authored shape change that a single static texture cannot supply. Normal-alpha colour layers retain deliberate hue; narrow additive cores provide brief light. Linear colour processing and consistent premultiplication matter before tuning brightness. Sparks align their length to velocity and lose speed to drag. Key poses must read without effects; mesh weights preserve planted feet and stable faces, while ears, tails and weapons follow later. A ground-attached shadow anchors the body. These are methods to test, not proof of quality in our battle.
