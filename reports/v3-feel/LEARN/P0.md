# P0: preserve work and enforce drawn poses

Question: How can the checker reject a moving idle drawing without discarding good unfinished CRAFT work or weakening old gates?

## Professional method
- Preserve existing measured failures, then add the new contract independently (P0-local).
- Use monotonic duration measurements and explicit clock origins; exact schedule timing is separate from video timing (P0-clock).
- Validate each pose, not an average that can conceal an individual defect (P0-experiment).
- Require identity, anchors and filmed readability alongside silhouette distance; moved geometry can achieve a low IoU without a new drawing (P0-experiment).
- Missing artifacts and missing required poses remain open rather than passing vacuously.
- Test positive, negative, missing and boundary cases (P0-test).
- Ignore patterns do not untrack existing files; remove only explicit generated paths from the index if needed (P0-ignore/P0-rm).
- Keep the final compact review evidence and preserve failure history (P0-local).
