## Professional method and local implications

- Direct attention from caster to travel to contact, with one dominant idea at each phase (Q3-T11; Q3B-S02).
- Block a readable silhouette before adding glow or many small particles (Q3-T11; Q3-T17).
- Give cast, body, impact and outro different contours and finite envelopes; a theme alone does not connect them (Q3B-S02).
- Use independent attachment/fragment delays after the main impulse, while keeping the action clock fixed (Q3-T11; D6/D7 reviews).
- Keep the broad body behind actors, and reserve foreground accents for sparse local contact details (D5/X16; Q3-T28).
- Update the existing scene through seekable uniforms and reused geometry; avoid a second full-canvas CPU composition path (Q3-T01/T19; failed QR-P3).
- Group render order can override child sorting; separate ground, rear body and foreground roots explicitly (Q3B-S01; D3/X09).
- Keep source paint in sRGB, calculations in linear light and output conversion single (Q3-T36).
- Apply authored local deformation before the existing opaque-bound normalization so Q2 remains the authority for painted body height (Q3B-L01).
- Artistically review the real ten-unit composition; an isolated mask does not prove readability or contact (Q3-T17; user section3).
