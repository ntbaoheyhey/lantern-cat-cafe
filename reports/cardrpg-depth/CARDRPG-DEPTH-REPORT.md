## Decisions made during implementation

| Choice | Reason and implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Three equipped skills from four per cat | Explicit pre-run trade-off. Level six or three stars unlocks the ultimate. A larger per-cat pool remains future depth work. |
| Five team energy and personal charge | Offensive, support and control skills compete within one numbered round. Defend and Pour energy each spend the actor's action. |
| Flavour advantage adds one break pressure | A visible rule rewards reading enemy intent. Broken signature attacks become interruptible. |
| Four equipment slots | Preserves all 48 earlier IDs and creates competition for two/four-piece sets and the Relic slot. |
| Single retained battle scene | Fixed decision/round heights preserve drawing-buffer dimensions through encounter transitions. |
| Capped currencies and repeated sinks | 420 fish points, 90 threads, 180 stamps and six essence per day. Legendary tailoring consumes a boss seal. |
| Existing idle timing | Eight-hour cap, 50% offline efficiency and two services share normal currency allowances. |
| Small read-only Relic effects | One selected holding replaces the lead cat's ordinary relic slot. Quantity has constant power. |
| Versioned plaintext save codes | FNV checksum catches accidental corruption. The app saves locally after commands. The file uses manual codes. |

All decisions and their test references are recorded in the design documents. UI calls pure seeded rules for outcomes. Numbered rounds grant each living eligible actor one action, with integer initiative used for order.
