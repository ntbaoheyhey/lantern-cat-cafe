# Lantern look checklist

Score each applicable row 1–10 at 1440×900 and 1280×720 from inspected browser captures. N/A requires an explicit reason. A screen is unfinished if any applicable row is below 7; Recruit, Map and Battle must also average at least 8. Scores are an agent visual review, not player validation.

| # | Concrete visual check |
|---|---|
| 01 | Cream, amber and warm wood dominate; night blue supports the scene without a dark-teal slab. |
| 02 | Character art is large, sharp and immediately identifiable on cards and screen headers. |
| 03 | Common grey, Rare blue, Epic purple and Legendary gold frames remain distinct without relying on text alone. |
| 04 | Rarity frames have crafted corners/ornament; Legendary cards have a subtle animated shine. |
| 05 | Owned cards have legible level badges and stars; unowned cards communicate how to recruit. |
| 06 | The primary action is chunky, high contrast, softly glowing, and visibly presses on input. |
| 07 | Panels use depth, materials and purposeful composition rather than identical dashboard boxes. |
| 08 | Currency/reward feedback has icons, visible amounts and a short count-up. |
| 09 | Red dots only point to a currently possible action, using the shared badge selector. |
| 10 | Screen entrances slide/fade in 150–250 ms; reduced motion has an immediate equivalent. |
| 11 | Cafe crew silhouettes are each at least 25% of viewport height with gentle idle motion. |
| 12 | Empty/loading states contain a drawn cue and one clear action or status, without blank spaces. |
| 13 | Labels are short; important body text stays readable at 1280×720. |
| 14 | Primary actions and essential state fit above the fold at 1280×720, with no horizontal overflow. |
| 15 | Layout, scenery and focal point give each screen its own identity. |
| 16 | Recruitment visibly deals, teases rarity, flips, celebrates Epic+, and resolves duplicates into shards. |
| 17 | Campaign is a scenic winding route with pulsing current stop, cleared stars, boss art and milestone chests. |
| 18 | Battle portraits, HP damage trails, rage readiness and boss phases are clear at a glance. |
| 19 | Ultimate cut-in uses big character art and readable skill name; speed and skip remain responsive. |
| 20 | Results reveal stars/rewards in order and give a clear Next/Retry/cheap upgrade action. |
