## Evidence and limits

- [Google Play interview with AFK Journey producer](https://play.google.com/store/apps/editorial?hl=en_GB&id=mc_games_editorialmd_best_game_afk_journey_interview_fcp): supports intended joy, adventure and an easy-going world; not exact screen layouts or retention claims.
- [Neko Atsume official how-to](https://www.nekoatsume.com/sp/en/about.html): explicitly presents observation, Catbook, Album, Shop and Remodel; no numerical timing claim.
- [web.dev animation guide](https://web.dev/articles/animations-guide): transform/opacity avoid much layout/paint work; unnecessary layer promotion can cost resources. Profile before treating an effect as cheap.
- [MDN prefers-reduced-motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@media/prefers-reduced-motion): detect and reduce/replace nonessential motion. Preserving reward state and input is our implementation requirement.
- Existing local studies carry the named game's system-level evidence. Psychological explanations here are design reasoning, not a causal user study. The saved LOOK-SCORES are agent judgements; real player enjoyment remains unverified.
