Ordinary hits reach the front; explicit pierce, area and backline skills retain their targeting. Legacy committed battle snapshots preserve their original rules and replays.

Existing three-unit A6 saves receive a fourth owned companion (Panko when needed).

The stage and preparation share `apps/web/src/game/cardrpg/stage/slots.json`. The unit height is 31% of the stage; rear seats are half a slot above the front seats; enemies mirror the party. Boss ratios are clamped to 1.7–1.95. Lower feet draw in front. Preparation supports tap swaps, drag swaps, bench substitution and saved teams.
