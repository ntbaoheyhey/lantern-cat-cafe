## Native Retina round-boundary correction

The first complete DPR 2 campaign failed with 13 long tasks of 51–98ms. A CPU profile repeat attributed 1.85 seconds of cumulative self time to renderer.setSize. Instrumented DOM rectangles showed that an empty current-round row collapsed from 34.09 to 24.64px, changing the canvas height by 9px; 99 callbacks included 98 real buffer-size changes. Skipping identical sizes alone did not fix this. The corrected layout reserves empty-row height and retains the round bar through the result; identical resize notifications also no longer reset the canvas. Final lifecycle checks require a single drawing-buffer size from start to finish and record requested versus adaptive effective quality. Requested quality remains Medium. Effective quality changes from Medium to Low at 53.41 seconds of page time; one drawing-buffer size is retained.
