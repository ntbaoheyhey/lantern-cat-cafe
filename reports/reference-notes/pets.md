# Companion collection and passive rewards

Evidence: archived `pages/wiki__pets.json` and the earlier systems proposal.
Live check: [companion page](https://tbhindex.com/wiki/pets), 2026-11-02.

## Unlock and ownership model

The reference presents companions as a small collection with persistent
account bonuses. Some unlock through lifetime kills of a target enemy,
while others use a paid-content entitlement. The observed kill requirements
span 500–5,000. Progress is described as accumulating across difficulties;
changing the farming location does not restart the lifetime counter.

The captured explanation says owned companions contribute together.
It does not describe choosing a single equipped pet, randomizing its
stats, breeding, feeding, rank upgrades, or pet attacks. Do not infer those
mechanics from a companion portrait or animation.

## Bonus ranges and units

Bonus families improve ordinary reward-container acquisition, local-boss
container acquisition, experience return, and currency return.
Captured positive per-companion bonuses span 1–20%; smaller seasonal
rewards lie at the low end, while some paid entitlements affect several
categories. There is no evidence here that a pet changes the quality
distribution inside a container.

The archived rendered page shows percentages. The live static page emits
some raw stat amounts in a different scale, approximately ten times those
displayed percentages. They must not be read directly as percentage points.
The conversion is inferred by comparing matching categories, not verified
against an engine schema. The range above uses the archived display units.

The source describes summing owned bonuses within each category and feeding
resource effects into farming calculations. It does not fully establish how
those totals combine with every other multiplier or cap. Keep ownership,
incremental pet effects, and the final applied account total separate.

## Information useful to a player

A collection entry needs its unlock condition, current progress, bonus
category, and ownership state. Farming suggestions should show the target
enemy and expected progress per run. That ranking is different from
currency per hour or shortest clear time.

An account summary can explain the aggregate benefit without forcing the
player to add every card. Locked entries should remain inspectable, with
progress kept distinct from any hypothetical preview.

These observations support the default-off P2 companion work. They neither
select our unlock requirements nor establish a paid-content model for us.
