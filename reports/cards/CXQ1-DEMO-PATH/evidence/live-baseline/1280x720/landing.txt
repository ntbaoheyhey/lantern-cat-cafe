How to play
1
Mochi the cat naps and plays on the strip, earning Fish and Yarn even while you're away (up to 8 h).
2
Beat the boss to earn one Cat Key per day (5 fights a day).
3
Spend Fish on gear upgrades in Inventory, and equip better drops.
4
Forge a Relic with 30 Yarn and 1 Cat Key, and mint it to your wallet on Avalanche Fuji (free testnet, costs no real money).
5
Trade your minted Relics with other players in Market.

Demo mode works without a wallet; sign in to save progress and mint Relics on the Avalanche Fuji testnet (free testnet, costs no real money). Wallet sign-in needs Chrome, Brave or Edge.
