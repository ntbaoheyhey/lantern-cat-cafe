# Original card RPG presentation assets

Twelve 640×800 transparent portraits are rendered from our three original hero GLBs; role variants share miniature families. No reference art, downloaded models/textures, samples, or image/model calls.

- Portrait pixels, parameters, tools, scripts, model/source hashes: assets/cardrpg/portraits/manifest.json.
- Named animation clips and every GLB hash: docs/turn3d/ASSET-MANIFEST.json.
- Motion, effects, audio, and account-gear mapping source hashes: assets/cardrpg/procedural-manifest.json.
- Portrait renderer: tools/cardrpg-demo/portrait.html and portraits.mjs.
- Evidence contact sheets: tools/cardrpg-demo/distinctness.py (Pillow, HPND).

Art license CC0-1.0. Three.js and React MIT; TypeScript Apache-2.0. Rendered rarity frames use the rules' common/rare/epic/legendary palette. No generative references or third-party art.
